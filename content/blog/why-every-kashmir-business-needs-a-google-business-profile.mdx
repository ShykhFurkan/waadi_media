When someone in Srinagar looks for "best bakery near me" or a traveller arriving in Anantnag searches for "car rental Kashmir", what do they look at first?

They do not read page two of search results. They look at the Google Maps pack: the three business listings that appear at the very top of Google, complete with phone numbers, photos, directions, and reviews.

If your business is not listed there, you are losing customers to competitors who are, often without realizing it.

A Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business) is a free tool provided by Google that lets local business owners manage how their business appears across Google Search and Maps.

## Why it matters so much in Kashmir

1. **Mobile-first local search:** The overwhelming majority of searches in Kashmir happen on Android smartphones. A customer tapping a "Call" or "Directions" button on Google Maps is ready to buy.
2. **Tourism credibility:** Travellers visiting Gulmarg, Pahalgam, or Sonamarg rely on verified Google listings to choose trustworthy tour operators, hotels, and restaurants.
3. **Zero advertising cost:** Ranking well in local search brings consistent enquiries without paying for every click.

## How to set up your profile properly

- **Claim your exact business name:** Use your real registered business name. Avoid stuffing extra keywords into the title, as Google's guidelines can penalize this.
- **Set accurate business hours:** Especially during seasonal shifts or winter months, keep your operating hours accurate.
- **Add high quality photos:** Photos of your storefront, products, team, or workspace help build immediate trust.
- **Collect genuine reviews:** Ask happy customers to leave an honest review on Google. Always reply politely to reviews.
