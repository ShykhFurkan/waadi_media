Before you hire a web design agency in Kashmir, you must know who owns the code, how much maintenance costs, and how the site performs on a budget Android phone. Asking the right practical questions upfront prevents unexpected expenses and ensures your website brings real customer enquiries.

Hiring someone to build your business website can feel intimidating without a technical background. Many business owners in Srinagar and Anantnag pay for a site only to discover months later that they cannot update basic details or that the agency retains ownership of the domain.

Here are seven straightforward questions to ask before you sign a contract or transfer a deposit.

## Quick summary

- Ask who legally owns the domain name, hosting account, and source code once the final invoice is paid.
- Confirm whether the website is built mobile-first and tested on real 4G mobile connections typical in Kashmir.
- Clarify all ongoing fees, including hosting, annual domain renewals, and software maintenance, before starting.
- Find out exactly who writes the content and optimizes the photos for search engines.
- Ensure the quote includes foundational on-page search engine setup, not just visual page layouts.

## 1. Who will legally own the domain name, code, and accounts?

This is the single most important question you can ask, yet it is the one most often skipped. 

Some agencies register the client's domain name under their own master account and host the website on an internal server that you cannot access. If you ever decide to part ways, you may find that you do not actually own your domain name or website files. Transferring them away can turn into an expensive dispute.

When working with a trustworthy [web design agency in Kashmir](/web-design-agency-kashmir), all core digital assets should be registered in your name or transferred to you upon completion. This includes:

- The domain name registration through a recognized registrar.
- The web hosting or cloud hosting dashboard.
- The source code, assets, and database.
- Any connected business accounts, such as Google Analytics or your Google Business Profile.

## 2. Is this website custom-built or based on a pre-made template?

There is nothing wrong with using templates for simple, low-cost projects. A basic single landing page starting from ₹5,000 on our [transparent pricing page](/pricing) can help a new workshop get online quickly. 

However, you should always know what you are paying for. Some developers charge ₹30,000 or ₹40,000 while quietly installing a bloated pre-made theme from an online marketplace. These off-the-shelf themes often come packed with dozens of unnecessary scripts and heavy plugins that slow down loading times on mobile networks.

Ask the agency directly:
- Are you designing this layout around my business goals, or modifying a pre-existing theme?
- What underlying framework or platform are you using?
- How many external plugins will the site require to function?

If you are trying to decide between platforms, reading our guide on [WordPress or a custom website](/blog/wordpress-or-a-custom-website-which-is-right-for-your-business) will help you understand the tradeoffs in performance, security, and long-term maintenance.

## 3. How will the website perform on a mid-range phone over mobile data?

Most web designers build and test websites on large desktop monitors connected to high-speed office broadband. But in Kashmir, your real customers are browsing on mid-range Android phones using 4G or 5G mobile connections while on the move.

If a travel agency page or a handicraft catalog takes eight seconds to display its first picture, visitors will tap the back button and visit a competitor. Google also uses mobile load speeds as a direct ranking signal.

Ask the designer:
- What mobile testing devices or screen sizes do you test against?
- Do you compress images automatically into modern formats like WebP or AVIF?
- What performance score or load time targets do you aim for on Google PageSpeed Insights?

A well-engineered website should load cleanly in under two seconds, even on modest mobile hardware.

## 4. What is included in the price, and what costs extra?

To avoid surprises, request an itemized scope of work in writing before work begins. A quote that simply says "Website Development: ₹25,000" leaves too much room for misunderstanding.

Verify whether the quote includes:
- Writing or polishing page copy in clear English.
- Sourcing or formatting product and location photos.
- On-page search optimization (meta titles, meta descriptions, and clean headings).
- Contact form integration with email or WhatsApp alerts.
- Basic security configuration and SSL certificate setup.

## 5. How easy is it for our team to update text and photos later?

Businesses evolve. You will introduce new products, adjust seasonal tour package rates, change your shop timings, or hire new team members. You should not have to pay a developer every time you need to correct a spelling error or update a phone number.

Ask whether the agency includes a user-friendly Content Management System (CMS) or an admin panel. Ask for a brief video walkthrough of how text editing works. 

If a website requires editing raw code or opening specialized software just to update a paragraph, you will eventually stop updating it, and the website will become outdated.

## 6. What foundational SEO steps are included before launch?

A beautiful website is useless if nobody can find it on search engines. Basic on-page search engine optimization (SEO) is not an advanced luxury; it is a fundamental part of responsible web design.

Ask whether the agency will:
- Research relevant keywords that local customers actually search for.
- Write unique meta titles and meta descriptions for every page.
- Set up an automated XML sitemap and submit it to Google Search Console.
- Configure Schema structured data so search engines understand your address, opening hours, and phone number.
- Ensure your business address matches your Google Business Profile.

If an agency tells you that SEO is an entirely separate service that requires a large monthly fee before they will even add basic page titles, consider looking elsewhere. Standard on-page SEO should be baked into every modern [website design and development](/services/website-design-development) project from day one.

## 7. What happens if something breaks after launch?

Technology changes continuously. Browsers update, software libraries require security patches, and contact forms occasionally run into email delivery issues.

Before paying a deposit, make sure you understand the post-launch support terms:
- Is there a warranty period (such as 30 days) during which bugs are fixed free of charge?
- Who handles domain renewals and server security updates?
- If you need a new page added later, what is the cost? (At Waadi Media, extra pages are transparently priced at ₹1,000 per page).
- How quickly does the team respond when an issue occurs?

A responsible partner will clearly explain their support terms so you have peace of mind long after your site goes live.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much should a standard business website cost in Kashmir?
Pricing depends on the scope of the project. A single-page landing page typically starts at ₹5,000, while a complete 5-page business website starts at ₹15,000. E-commerce stores with product catalogs and online payment gateways start from ₹25,000. Always ensure the quote includes design, mobile responsiveness, and foundational SEO.

### How long does it take to design and launch a website?
A focused landing page can be completed in 3 to 5 business days if text and photos are ready. A standard 5 to 7 page business website typically takes 2 to 3 weeks. E-commerce stores or custom software projects usually take 3 to 5 weeks. Delays most commonly happen when waiting for content or photography from the client.

### Should I pay the entire project fee upfront?
No. Standard industry practice is a 50% deposit to begin work, with the remaining 50% paid upon delivery and approval before final launch. For larger custom software projects, payments are usually split across milestones agreed upon in writing. Never pay 100% upfront to an agency or freelancer you have not worked with before.

### Can an agency guarantee the number one spot on Google?
No agency or developer can legitimately guarantee a specific ranking on Google. Google’s search algorithms evaluate hundreds of factors that no third party controls. A dependable agency will focus on clean code, fast mobile load speeds, relevant content, and proper local SEO rather than making unrealistic ranking promises.
