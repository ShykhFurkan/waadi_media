Many young entrepreneurs in Kashmir ask us: *"I already have 5,000 followers on Instagram and post reels regularly. Do I really need to spend money on a website?"*

It is a fair question. Instagram is visual, popular, and free to create. For showcasing products and catching attention, social media works remarkably well.

However, relying entirely on an Instagram page creates significant vulnerabilities for any serious business. Here is why the most successful businesses use both.

## The risks of relying only on social media

### 1. You don't own your social media audience
Algorithms change constantly. An account can be shadowbanned, reported, or restricted without warning, cutting off your primary channel to customers overnight. A website is your own digital property that you control 100%.

### 2. Search intent is different
People browse Instagram when they are bored or looking for inspiration. People use Google when they have an immediate problem to solve: *"book cab from Srinagar to Gulmarg"* or *"apple wholesale suppliers Kashmir"*. When a customer is searching on Google with intent to buy, an Instagram profile rarely ranks.

### 3. Friction in ordering and payments
Answering 50 DMs a day asking *"price please?"*, sending UPI QR codes manually, and typing out delivery addresses is exhausting and leads to lost orders. A website automates your product catalogue, captures delivery addresses accurately, and takes payments automatically 24 hours a day.

## How they work best together

The ideal setup is not choosing between a website or Instagram; it is connecting them:
- Use Instagram and short reels to build awareness and showcase your brand.
- Direct serious buyers to your website link in bio, where they can browse your full catalogue, view transparent pricing, and place orders or book a call.
