## The basic breakdown

Website pricing depends on three main things: how many pages you need, whether you need custom features or e-commerce, and who is building it.

### 1. Single landing page (From ₹5,000)
A single-page website is ideal for local service providers, workshop owners, or events who want a clean, professional web presence fast.
- **What is included:** Hero section, services overview, about section, contact form, direct WhatsApp chat, and Google Maps location.
- **Timeline:** Typically 3 to 5 business days.
- **Who it is for:** New businesses testing a concept, single-service specialists, or anyone needing a modern business card on the internet.

### 2. Business website with 5 to 7 pages (From ₹15,000)
A complete multi-page website is the standard for established local businesses.
- **What is included:** Home, About, Services, Individual Service Pages, Portfolio or Gallery, and Contact page. Built mobile-first with fast load speeds and complete on-page SEO.
- **Timeline:** Typically 2 to 3 weeks.
- **Who it is for:** Tour operators, clinics, schools, consultancies, and retailers who need customers to explore what they offer.

### 3. CMS-based website (From ₹25,000)
If your business regularly posts news, updates tour packages, or publishes articles, a Content Management System (CMS) allows your team to edit text, images, and pages without writing code.
- **What is included:** Full visual design, structured content collections, automated image optimization, and personal handover training for your staff.
- **Timeline:** Typically 3 to 4 weeks.

### 4. E-commerce store (From ₹35,000)
Kashmir produces saffron, dry fruits, pashmina shawls, wood carvings, and papier-mâché that customers across India and abroad want to buy.
- **What is included:** Product catalog with search and categories, direct UPI, debit card, credit card, and net banking payment gateways, automated shipping integration, customer WhatsApp order notifications, and product SEO.
- **Timeline:** Typically 3 to 5 weeks.

## What affects the final price?

- **Content readiness:** If you already have your text, high-resolution photographs, and logo ready, development goes faster and costs less.
- **Custom integrations:** Adding custom booking engines, CRM systems, or automated customer notifications requires additional engineering hours.
